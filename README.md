## 👋 About Me
🚀 Computer Vision and Deep Learning Researcher exploring intelligent systems for visual understanding and real-world AI applications.
 
I am a Computer Vision and Deep/Machine Learning Researcher with 4 years of research experience in developing AI-based solutions for challenging visual recognition problems. My research interests include Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Machine Learning, Object Detection, Image Processing, and Data-Efficient Learning, with a focus on building robust models that can understand complex visual information.
 
Throughout my research journey, I have worked on designing and implementing computer vision pipelines, including image preprocessing, model development, training, and evaluation. My work involves applying deep learning approaches to visual recognition tasks while exploring methods to improve model accuracy, robustness, and generalization.
 
My technical expertise includes Python, OpenCV, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, MATLAB, and Pandas, with experience in developing machine learning workflows for image-based applications. I am particularly interested in deep neural networks, CNNs, object detection, document image analysis, OCR, representation learning, and intelligent vision systems.
 
My research contribution includes a peer-reviewed conference publication at IEEE ICEE 2024, demonstrating my ability to conduct and communicate research in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision.
 
I welcome connections with researchers and academics working in Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence, and Machine Learning.
